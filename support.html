Common answers

  • Delete your account: in the app — Profile → Settings → Delete account. Removes everything, immediately.
  • Report someone: tap the "…" button on any chat, plan, or join request. Reports are private.
  • Block or unblock: block from the same "…" button; undo it in Settings → Blocked people.
  • Location off? Roam works fine — just type any city when building a plan.
  • Replay the app tour: Settings → Show me around.
