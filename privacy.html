Roam — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026 · Rudd Media LLC

Roam builds travel itineraries and, if you choose, lets other people ask to join individual stops on your plan. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and who else sees it. If something here is unclear, email jakerudd629@yahoo.com.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it lives
Approximate location To work out which city you're in so a plan is one tap away Used on your device. We convert it to a city name; we do not store your coordinates or keep any location history.
Account email To sign you in and keep your plans across devices Our database. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we only ever see Apple's relay address.
Instagram username and public profile photo Optional. It shows on join requests so a host can see you're a real person before approving Our database. We only read what is already public. We never ask for your Instagram password and cannot post anything.
Plans you publish (city, times, venues, stops) So they can appear to other travelers and be joined Our database, visible to signed-in users while the plan is upcoming. Plans you don't publish stay on your phone.
Join requests and messages To run the request/approve flow and the chat that follows Our database, visible only to the host and approved joiners of that stop.
Subscription status To unlock paid features Handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Anthropic — generates itineraries. We send the city, your available time, budget and chosen interests. We do not send your name, email or precise location.
  • Google Maps Platform — venue photos, coordinates and city search.
  • Apple — sign-in and all payments.
  • RevenueCat — records which subscription you hold.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your account

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, published plans, join requests and messages. It cannot be undone. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Children

Roam is not intended for anyone under 17, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll update the date above and note it in the app.
